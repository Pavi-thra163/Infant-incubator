OBJECTIVE:
     
                         The main objective of low-cost, heater-free infant incubator with power backup is provide a safe and controlled environment for infants especially for  premature and critically ill babies by continuously regulating and monitoring essential parameters. It ensures that vital conditions such as temperature, humidity, and oxygen concentration remains stable, help the infant’s body to adapt and grow. At the same time, the system tracks the baby’s health indicators ,including heart rate, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, and body temperature, so that any abnormal changes can be detected at an early stage. In addition, the system enhances infant safety by identifying faults like overheating, power loss, or sensor faults.
